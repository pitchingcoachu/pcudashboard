01

MOVEMENT
PREP.

5–6 movement and mobility exercises that target your deficiencies.

Focus Areas
T-spine rotation, hip mobility, and whatever else your assessment flags as limited.
02

DYNAMIC
WARM UP.

Elevate your heart rate with athletic, dynamic, full-body movements.

Movement Pool
World's Greatest Stretch, lunge variations, hops, skips, jumps, sprints.
03

PRE-THROW
ARM CARE.

Total arm activation before a single ball gets thrown.

Routine
Arm circles, band routine, plyo ball drop catches, shoulder tube.
04

PRE-THROW
PLYOS.

3–5 plyo drills that prepare you to throw and focus on your delivery deficiencies.

Progression
Mainly overload balls, low-to-medium intent. Flow from most constrained to least constrained drills.
05

THROWING.

Extension long toss out to 150'+ with 4–6 pulldowns on the way in at 90%+ effort.

Finish with light grip work.

06

BULLPEN.

Have a plan of attack based on your player plan goals.

The Standard Buckets
Command, pitch design, velo, and mechanics.
07

POST-THROW
ARM CARE.

Restore lost range of motion and begin the recovery process.

Focus Areas
Posterior shoulder, scaps, and forearm/elbow.
08

LIFTING.

Based on your specific plan — could be a lower split, upper split, or a full body lift.
