The exact routine we use before every bullpen.

1. Movement Prep — 5-6 mobility exercises for your deficiencies (t-spine, hips, etc.)
2. Dynamic Warm Up — full-body athletic movements to elevate heart rate
3. Pre-Throw Arm Care — arm circles, bands, plyo drop catches, shoulder tube
4. Pre-Throw Plyos — 3-5 drills, low-to-medium intent, most to least constrained
5. Throwing — extension long toss to 150'+, 4-6 pulldowns at 90%+ effort
6. Bullpen — plan of attack: command, pitch design, velo, mechanics
7. Post-Throw Arm Care — posterior shoulder, scaps, forearm/elbow
8. Lifting — lower split, upper split, or full body based on your plan
