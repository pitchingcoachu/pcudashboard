Whiffs get the attention. Called strikes win the same count.

CSW% combines both:
(Called Strikes + Whiffs) / Total Pitches

MLB average: 28.2%
Mason Miller: 40.5%

The location takeaway:
• Bigger shapes—especially curveballs and sweepers—generally create more takes.
• Locate opposite the pitch's movement direction to generally create more takes.
• Locate with the pitch's movement direction to generally create more swings.

CSW% is not just about stuff. It connects pitch shape, location, and hitter decisions.
